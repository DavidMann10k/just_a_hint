## Hero

```text

Invent an entirely different original composition, environment and generic traveler. Do not reuse, trace, paint over or reconstruct the reference's characters, armor, cities, mountains, architecture, flying craft or arrangement. No Blizzard logos, Warcraft logos, faction emblems or identifiable game characters.

Brand idea: restrained quest assistance that lets the player remain immersed in the world; the promise is exploration and a small hint when requested. Convey quiet curiosity and room to wander. Beautiful, confident, warm fantasy illustration rather than a loud action advertisement.

Scene: A narrow, unmarked earthen trail disappears around a bend into an original wooded mountain valley at sunset. Dark plum foreground rocks and broad windswept pines frame an open view of hazy blue-violet foothills, patches of amber grasses, copper-green trees and a warm peach sky. A single small generic cloaked traveler on the right third, seen from behind, pauses with a folded parchment in one hand; simple weathered clothing and a modest pack, no weapons or elaborate armor. The traveler occupies under 12 percent of the image height. No cities, battles, creatures or famous landmarks. Plenty of rich visible painterly texture; broad deliberate shapes and fine edges only where needed. Saturated yet natural color, atmospheric depth, no photorealistic rendering.

Brand layout: Keep the left 45 percent quieter and darker for highly legible typography. In the upper-middle left, place a small distinctive emblem: one slim asymmetric gold direction needle angled toward the upper right, inside a fine incomplete antique-brass circle. Only one needle; no compass letters, ticks, cardinal points, exclamation marks or route lines. The circle has a small gap, suggesting just enough direction rather than complete navigation. Elegant, flat readable emblem, not a realistic round compass object.
Below or beside this emblem place the exact large title:
"Just a Hint"
Use an original confident literary fantasy serif, title case, warm parchment ivory with very subtle aged-gold warmth, balanced spacing, no huge beveled metal, no outlines or copying the Warcraft wordmark. Title is dominant and reads easily when scaled down.
Below the title, use small clean warm-ivory typography, exact text:
"FOR WORLD OF WARCRAFT: FOREVER"
and a smaller second line:
"UNOFFICIAL ADDON"
The addon name must be far more prominent than the game compatibility label. Keep all text and emblem within a generous safe area, at least 8 percent inward from the image edges. Ensure every word is spelled exactly as supplied.

Constraints: Original artwork inspired by the reference's painterly art language only. Preserve wide composition and the quiet left typography zone. Cohesive warm gold / parchment / plum / indigo palette. The world should be the subject, with the traveler and gold needle only small clues. No extra words, slogan, labels, UI, quest pins, glowing trails, cartoon question marks, fake screenshots, watermarks, heavy ornamental frames or endorsement claims. Opaque background.
```
